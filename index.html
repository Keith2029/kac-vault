ABOUT THE VAULT

A collection becomes more valuable when its story is organized.

KAC Vault is being built as both a family collection archive and a practical business platform. The first stage is preservation and cataloging. The same structure can later support QR labels, market tracking and e-commerce without rebuilding the collection from scratch.
